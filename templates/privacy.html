Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect only the information you provide directly: your name, email address, and the expense entries you choose to log. We do not collect payment card details or banking credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide and improve the Spendly experience — displaying your expense history, generating summaries, and ensuring secure access to your account.

3. Data Storage

All data is stored in encrypted SQLite databases with foreign keys enabled. We do not share your information with advertising networks or data brokers.

4. Third Party Services

We rely on Google Fonts for typography and standard web infrastructure. These services may collect anonymous usage data but do not receive your financial records.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or your data, please reach out through the app or contact our support team.
